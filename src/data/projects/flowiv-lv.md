## Problem

FlowIV LV needed an official website that could present its mobile IV therapy services clearly, support booking intent, and give local Las Vegas customers enough context to choose the right treatment.

## My Role

I was hired to build the site and currently continue to work with FlowIV LV. My work covers the responsive site experience, service presentation, content structure, and the front-end implementation that supports their live business presence.

## Technical Decisions

- Built with Astro, TypeScript, and Tailwind for a fast, maintainable business website.
- Structured service pages and calls to action around mobile users who are comparing treatments or ready to book.
- Prioritized clear information hierarchy so treatment details remain scannable without feeling sparse.

## Impact

The site is the official FlowIV LV web presence and supports real customer discovery, service education, and booking intent for the business.

## What I Would Improve Next

- Continue refining local search content as services and coverage areas evolve.
- Add deeper conversion tracking around booking paths.
- Expand reusable content patterns for new treatment pages and business updates.
